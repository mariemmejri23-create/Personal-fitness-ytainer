If you’re new here or wondering what to expect, these answers will guide you through how coaching works, what’s included, and how we tailor every plan to your needs.

What should I expect during my first session?

We start with a relaxed conversation about your goals, history and schedule, followed by a short movement assessment. You leave with clear first steps — no pressure, no judgement.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Programs can be built for a gym, a home setup with minimal equipment, or a mix of both.

How often should I train each week?

Most clients train 3–4 times per week. Your plan is adapted to your energy, recovery and lifestyle.

Is nutrition coaching included?

Yes. Every package includes nutrition guidance, from simple fundamentals to fully personalised plans.

Can I cancel or pause my plan anytime?

Yes, you can pause or cancel at any time. Life happens — your plan should flex with it.

How long will it take to see results?

Most clients feel more energy within 2–3 weeks and see visible changes within 6–8 weeks of consistent training.
